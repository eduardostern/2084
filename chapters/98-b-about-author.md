# About the Author

**Eduardo H. Stern** was born in São Paulo in 1976. His journey into computing began at six, when he inherited his late brother Marcelo’s TK-82C — a Brazilian ZX-81 clone with 1KB of RAM and a membrane keyboard. Marcelo died at seventeen; every program Eduardo has written since carries that legacy.

He taught himself Z80 assembly from photocopied magazines, hex-edited cassette tapes, and built his first PC programs in Turbo Pascal and C. Forty years later he has shipped real-time trading systems, medical databases, genomics pipelines, and embedded sensor networks — always from the ground up, always asking what the machine is actually doing.

*2084* is his first novel. He wrote it the same way he writes code: human vision directing, AI drafting, human reviewing every line. The ideas are his. The words arrived from somewhere in between.

He lives in São Paulo with his wife, Giovana. You can reach him at eduardostern@icloud.com.
